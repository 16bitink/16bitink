Game numbers (J, M): car trips per household per day 3 to 4 (two-car households 5 to 6). Purpose shares for game spawn: work 25%, school/nursery 10%, shopping/errands 20%, leisure/social 25%, escort/other 10%, home-bound remainder. Refuel: sim car refuels when tank under 25%; typical interval every 5 to 8 days (weekly in sprawl, 10+ days in dense/short-trip cities). Trip chains (J, M, widely reported in travel research): about 1 in 3 workers drops a child before work; about 1 in 3 evening trips stops at a shop or errand on the way home. Model as: morning home to school to work; evening work to shop to home, with chain probability 30% for households with children.

## Saffron Bay (UAE / Gulf)

| Item | Value | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Weekend | Sat-Sun; Fri half day for government (07:30-12:00); Sharjah-style Fri-Sun in some emirates. Private firms vary | D, H |
| Work | Gov 8 h Mon-Thu; private 08:00-17:00 or 09:00-18:00; split shifts in retail/hospitality (10-14, 16-20) about 15% | D (gov), J (split) |
| Flexible/remote | Studies cite about 30% morning peak cut if 2 h start window + 4-5 remote days/month; assume 10% flexible | D+J, M |
| School | Start 07:30-07:45 typical (some 09:00); finish about 14:00-15:00; staggering 06:45 under study | D, M |
| School travel | Car and school bus dominant (judgment: 60% car, 30% bus, 10% walk) | J, L |
| Peaks | Morning 06:30-09:00 (heaviest 06-08), school pick-up wave from 14:00; evening 17:00-19:30 | D (anecdotal)+J, M |
| Lunch | Mostly out or delivered; midday heat keeps people indoors | J, M |
| Shopping | Malls open about 10:00-24:00; busiest after 18:00 and weekend evenings | J, M |
| Dinner / cafe / night | Dinner 20:00-22:30; cafes and walks to 01:00 in cooler months | J, M |
| Season | Summer midday (12-16) near-empty outdoors; beach and parks only dawn/after 18:00; winter outdoor weekends peak | J, H |
| Weekend | Brunch, mall, beach (winter), family visits, desert trips | J, M |
| Fuel | Cheap fuel, weekly fills; stations busy 17-20 and Fri-Sat; EV share low (keep under 10%) | J, L |
| Trucks | Heavy trucks restricted from peaks in city roads (typical rule); night deliveries 22-05 | J, M |
| Calendar | School holidays July-August (many residents leave), shopping festivals Jan and Dec | J, M |

Sources: [UAE weekend](https://time.com/6126260/uae-working-days-weekend/), [u.ae gov hours](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/Sector-of-employment/working-in-uae-government-sector/working-hours-in-the-public-sector), [Dubai school traffic](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/uae/transport/school-traffic-rush-peak-hour-morning-runs), [staggered schools](https://www.thenationalnews.com/uae/education/2021/09/07/dubai-schools-look-at-staggered-start-times-as-heavy-traffic-returns/). Confidence: M.

**5 differences a player notices:** (1) Dead midday in summer, evening rush of cars to malls. (2) School-run jam at 07:00 is the biggest peak, before work. (3) Late dinner traffic past 21:00. (4) Fri/Sat-Sun weekend flip: Monday-Thursday commute only. (5) Very few walkers, heavy delivery bikes and vans.
