## Script

[mischievously] Because right now… you're this. [surprised] A fish larva! Two tenths of an inch. [excited] You're going to grow, step by step, all the way up to that whale… and at every size, I'll tell you how long you'd last. [confident] Not guesses: every forecast is calculated from real measurements of how fast fish die… scaled to each size. [nervous] And watch for two things. At one size, the ocean almost stops hunting you… and it's not the biggest. [dramatically] And at the very top, the thing that nearly wiped you out… isn't even an ocean animal.

*Moods: awe → dread → curiosity → sly → shock → hype → trust → dread → mystery.*

*Frame 1: a blue whale's eye fills the right half, an orca fin cutting past in front; top-right a stopwatch with "YOUR FORECAST: ?". ### 2 · Step 1: the larva (0:35–1:30)

> [nervous] Okay. Two tenths of an inch, five millimetres… one thousandth of a gram. [chuckles] You're mostly eyes, and you're see-through. [sarcastic] Great camouflage. Terrible armour. [dramatically] Almost everything that hunts here is bigger than you. [curious] Some jellyfish swallow larvae like you… and digest them in about three hours. [serious] When scientists followed fish larvae day by day, they lost anywhere from one percent… to half of them. Every. Single. Day. [nervous] So… your forecast. [pause] Half of all fish your size are gone in twelve days. [shocked] And the chance you see next year? Three… in ten BILLION. [deadpan] If every person on Earth hatched as a larva like you… about two of us would make it. [warmly] Luckily, fish lay a LOT of eggs. [excited] So let's grow.

*Moods: dread → laugh → deadpan → epic → curiosity → gravity → suspense → shock → bigger shock → dark laugh → relief → hype.*

*A cloud of larvae (the field from `foodchain/field.ts`), the YOU arrow on one. A mauve stinger jellyfish (*Pelagia*) drifts through and a larva sticks to a tentacle; a small "2.5–3 h" digest timer, then the jelly drifts off (no gore). The stopwatch runs fast. On "half of them", a day counter flips and the cloud thins by half. Silence, then the stamps: "HALF GONE IN 12 DAYS · MODEL, STRETCHED" and "NEXT YEAR: 3 IN 10,000,000,000". On "two of us", a world globe with two tiny lights.*

Sources: size and weight are the ladder's (a typical early marine larva, round 1). Jellyfish: *Pelagia noctiluca* digests fish larvae in 2.5–3.0 h (Purcell et al. 2014, MEPS 510:201–213). Daily loss: 0.01–0.69 per day across 22 species, about 1–50% a day (Houde 1989, *Fishery Bulletin* 87:471–495). Forecast: Lorenzen 1996 at 0.001 g (median 11.5 days; e^-M ≈ 3 × 10^-10). "About two of us": 8.2 billion × 3 × 10^-10 ≈ 2.5.

[dramatically] Every time you get ten times heavier, your chance of dying each year… roughly halves. [warmly] A scientist found it by pooling studies of fish from oceans, lakes and rivers. Sources: tunas keep their muscles warmer than the water (regional endothermy; to be sourced). Orcas in the Strait of Gibraltar hunt bluefin, and 18 of 39 also take them from fishing lines (Esteban et al. 2016, *Biological Conservation* 194:31–38, doi:10.1016/j.biocon.2015.11.031); no prey size claimed. Forecast at 10 kg.

Sources: tiger sharks: "Prey, similar in size to humans, begin to occur in the diet… approximately 230 cm TL" (Lowe et al. 1996, *Environmental Biology of Fishes* 47:203–211, doi:10.1007/BF00005044; 281 Hawaiian tiger sharks). **Arguable line (comment test):** "The safest size on this whole ladder… is yours." It's arguable (a blue whale's model odds are higher; a person's real predation risk is near zero), never false.

Sources: ~360,000 blue whales killed 1904–1973 in the Southern Hemisphere and northern Indian Ocean: 345,775 Antarctic + 13,022 pygmy = 358,797 (Branch et al. 2008, IWC Scientific Committee paper SC/60/SH9, as given in a 2020 *Endangered Species Research* paper, doi:10.3354/esr01077). "Then the hunting stopped" and "climbing": to be confirmed (Branch 2004 growth rate).
